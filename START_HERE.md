## 📊 Common Polymarket Strategies (What I'm Looking For)

### Information Edge Strategy
**Indicators:**
- Large bets ($1k-$10k+)
- Specific category focus (politics OR sports OR crypto)
- Long holds (weeks/months)
- Early market entry

**Example:** Political analyst with superior polling data

### Market Making Strategy
**Indicators:**
- Both YES and NO positions
- High frequency (100+ trades/week)
- Many markets simultaneously
- Small spreads

**Example:** Providing liquidity for trading fees

### Arbitrage Strategy
**Indicators:**
- Very quick in/out
- Offsetting positions
- Cross-platform trades
- Small margins, high volume

**Example:** Trading price differences between platforms

### Odds Trading Strategy
**Indicators:**
- Short holds (hours/days)
- Doesn't wait for resolution
- Trades on volatility
- Technical analysis approach

**Example:** Buying when odds drop, selling when they rise
